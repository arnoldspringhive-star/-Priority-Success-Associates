Notice of Privacy Practices

Information regarding HIPAA compliance and how your health information is handled.

THIS NOTICE DESCRIBES HOW MEDICAL INFORMATION ABOUT YOU MAY BE USED AND DISCLOSED AND HOW YOU CAN GET ACCESS TO THIS INFORMATION. PLEASE REVIEW IT CAREFULLY.

Our Legal Duty
We are required by applicable federal and state law (including HIPAA and North Carolina state regulations) to maintain the privacy of your Protected Health Information (PHI). We are also required to give you this Notice about our privacy practices, our legal duties, and your rights concerning your PHI.

How We May Use and Disclose Health Information

  • Treatment: We may use or disclose your PHI to personnel who are involved in providing your healthcare.
  • Payment: We may use and disclose your PHI to obtain payment for services we provide to you.
  • Healthcare Operations: We may use and disclose your PHI in connection with our healthcare operations, including quality assessment and improvement activities.
  • Emergencies & Public Health: We may disclose your PHI to appropriate authorities in emergency situations, to prevent a serious threat to health or safety, or as required by law.

Your Patient Rights

  • Right to Access: You have the right to look at or get copies of your health information.
  • Right to Request Restrictions: You have the right to request that we place additional restrictions on our use or disclosure of your PHI.
  • Right to Amend: If you believe your health information is incorrect or incomplete, you have the right to request that we amend it.
  • Right to an Accounting of Disclosures: You have the right to receive a list of instances in which we or our business associates disclosed your PHI for purposes other than treatment, payment, healthcare operations, and certain other activities.
