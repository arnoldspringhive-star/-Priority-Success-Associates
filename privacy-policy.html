Privacy Policy

Information regarding how your data is collected, used, and protected.

Information We Collect
We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you express an interest in obtaining information about us or our services, or when you contact us. This may include your name, email address (e.g., via our contact forms), phone number, and any other information you choose to provide.

How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and fulfill your requests.
  • Send administrative information, such as appointment reminders.
  • Improve our website and services to better serve our community.

Data Security
We prioritize the security of your personal information. We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal data. However, please be aware that no electronic transmission over the internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Third-Party Services
We use third-party services (such as Formaloo) to process appointment requests. Please do not submit sensitive medical information through our website's general contact forms.
